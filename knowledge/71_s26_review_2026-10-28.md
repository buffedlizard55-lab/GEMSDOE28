### What the H33-2-B2 claim may represent — and what cannot be concluded

Historical repository forensic notes ([S20 result](57_s20_result.md), [S24 active-checkout review](68_active_checkout_review_2026-10-27.md)) describe H33-2-B2 as a pruning operation on a dotted H27-4 solo pattern: a reported 40,199-dot base lost 2,545 dots close to the catalogue and retained 37,654, with no added dots. These are historical repository geometry claims, not a file-bound organizer receipt; the exact H33-2 raster is not in this checkout, and older records disagree about score provenance/projection. The only defensible conclusion is that the supplied 0.2778 claim is the largest of the nine numbers supplied, not that its causal mechanism or organizer association has been verified.

The repo's DTI implementation uses `TPw / (TPw + 0.2 FPw + 0.8 FNw)` with a triangular 300 m kernel. Consequently, pruning is not automatically beneficial: removing a low-credit prediction can reduce false-positive mass, while removing a prediction near a withheld positive loses true-positive credit and increases false-negative cost. For context only, substituting the **USER-REPORTED / UNVERIFIED** 0.2778 into the code's algebraic marginal-inclusion condition gives a required incremental `dTP/dFP` ratio of about 0.0588 for an added set to improve that DTI. This 0.0588 is a derived decision threshold, **not a HOLDOUT-DTI, leaderboard score, or score estimate**. It illustrates why selective emission can matter, but it cannot explain this artifact's reported result without its exact bytes, evaluator, labels, and receipt. No validated method in this checkout is shown to beat the claim.

## 3–5 hypothesis screen, ranked before implementation

Four candidates are described in full in [`registry/s26_hypotheses.json`](../registry/s26_hypotheses.json). The ranking is qualitative and prioritizes actionable complementarity, cost, and data access; **expected improvement is unknown for all four**. No numeric DTI or leaderboard projection is made.

1. **S26-TCB — two-sided directional texture covariance** (top actionable candidate). Existing competition bands `rtp`, `tmi_hg`, and `cond_surf`; target opposite-side directional semivariogram/covariance change after local mean removal. Damage or alteration can change directional texture when broad amplitudes cancel. This differs from the implemented gradient/edge, Hessian/vesselness, and context methods. Main mimics: facies/lithologic boundaries, intrusions, leveling seams and resampling. Medium-high implementation cost; no new external data, but current cache is absent. **Not tested or authorized.**
2. **S26-MCR — spatially cross-fitted conductivity residual margins.** Existing `cond_surf`, `depth_to_base_surf`, `det_elev`, and `det_elev_slope`; target spatially unusual conductivity after conditioning on basement-depth and terrain context. This differs from raw conductivity edges and the existing clay-cap/dilation interactions. Main mimics: saline water, lacustrine clay, irrigation return flow and EM depth effects. Medium cost; no new external data. **Not tested or authorized.**
3. **S26-HFR2M — heat-flow residual × independent 2 m temperature-probe conjunction.** Needs USGS ScienceBase heat-flow residuals, GDR #1391 probes, and competition conductivity/depth. Main mimic: groundwater discharge or a warm aquifer without a fault. High cost. The official sources and historical runner-fetch evidence are recorded in the source ledger, but raw bytes are absent in this checkout and those hosts are outside the current runtime's allowed outbound-host list; **not viable for a holdout run here**.
4. **S26-DNT — drainage-network displacement and knickpoint trains.** Needs official USGS 3DEP 1 m DEM tiles; targets channel offsets, beheaded channels, wind gaps, and aligned knickpoints, unlike the repository's mainly amplitude-based LiDAR terrain operators. Main mimics: lithologic controls, landslides, artificial channels, and DEM artifacts. Very high cost. The official 3DEP catalog/collection is linked below, but this checkout lacks the challenge-specific tiles and USGS hosts are outside the runtime's allowed outbound-host list; **not viable for a holdout run here**.

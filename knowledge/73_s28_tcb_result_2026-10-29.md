## What was actually run

1. **Inputs:** the 32-column prepared feature cache was restored and hash-checked. Only `rtp`, `tmi_hg`, and `cond_surf` were read. The restored files are owner-supplied GitHub mirrors, not organizer-authenticated downloads.
2. **Operator:** each selected layer was filled only at source-nodata cells by normalized Gaussian interpolation, robustly standardized, and evaluated at fixed 2, 4, and 8 pixel scales. Four unoriented boundary normals compared tangent covariance on opposite sides after side-local mean removal. The three layer surfaces were fused by geometric mean. No catalogue feature entered the surface.
3. **Canaries:** each layer and the fused surface were tested alone. The leakage canary passed. The largest **individual fold** canary AUC was **0.5059894**; the largest pooled feature AUC in the run card was **0.4990656352**. Both are well below the 0.90 suspicion threshold. These are leakage diagnostics, not skill scores.
4. **Surface novelty before placement:** PASS against 28 distinct current selected artifacts from the nine requested owner repositories plus the calibration control; maximum absolute surface rank correlation was 0.0186062. This is a geometry audit, not a DTI or organizer score.
5. **Final-dot novelty:** STOP. The 37,654 in-memory dots had 1.0000000 directed within-3-pixel overlap with the spacing-five calibration control. The final-dot comparison covered all candidate dots. No TIFF writer was called.

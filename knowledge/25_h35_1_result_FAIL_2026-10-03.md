1. **Thermal conjunction does not enrich.** Added (0.04311 pooled) ≈ control (0.04415) ≈
   LOSFO base far-field (0.0465, `knowledge/20` §2). The four-way conjunction (hot well +
   alteration + sub-threshold ridge + Euler) selects dots worth no more than unselected
   far-field ridge at the same budget and spacing. The direction control fired correctly.
2. **The whole far-field ridge sits below the bar.** Even the base's far-field subset earns
   0.0527 absolute credit/dot (no spacing penalty) — below τ_live = 0.05485. Clearing the bar
   needs ≈ +25% enrichment over marginal base quality. That is the measured height of the ADD
   bar, and it applies to every future ADD arm, not just this one.
3. **The gate design is validated by the failure.** Cell DTI rose under BOTH arms (base 0.10067
   → added-union 0.10118 → control-union 0.10139) because the cell threshold (~0.0206) is far
   below the measured quality. A cell-threshold gate — the rule `knowledge/20` §3 originally
   proposed — would have PASSED both arms while they LOSE live DTI. Gating on τ_live
   (`knowledge/22` §3 correction) correctly fails them. This is now an empirical fact about the
   harness, not just arithmetic.
4. **ADD measurement is noisy at 2% budget.** Added seed-means range 0.0073–0.0733 (SD ≈ 0.024
   at ~460 dots/seed; credit is lumpy — a few dots near truth earn most). Any future ADD gate
   with 8/10-seed consistency needs either a large true effect or a bigger budget; the H35-4
   preregistration sizes its 3% budget from this measured noise (a power calculation on harness
   variance, disclosed there — not tuning, since H35-4's decade and constants are untouched).

# Why the 0.2477 file won, and what a higher score needs

Scores below are **owner-reported** (not DrivenData receipts). Model quantities are labelled *model*; nothing here is a measured leaderboard value
unless it says "owner-reported". Hidden-truth size (|G| about 12.6k px-equivalent) is the sibling's calibration from one lattice submission
(owner-reported 0.0904) and is **unconfirmed**; every derived figure inherits that uncertainty.

## 1. The metric, reduced to one line
With TP, FP the kernel-weighted true/false-positive masses (official definition, `src/gems27/metric.py`) and |G| the number of hidden truth pixels:

    DTI = TP / (TP + 0.2 FP + 0.8 FN) = TP / (0.2 TP + 0.2 FP + 0.8 |G|)           (FN = |G| - TP)

* FN is capped by |G|, so it cannot be driven below 0.8|G|; false positives are charged at 0.2 each but are unbounded - with ~54k emitted pixels they are
  the largest term of the denominator (about 49 % of it in the reconstruction below).
* **Inclusion rule (derived, tested in `tests/test_metric.py`):** adding a pixel set raises DTI iff  dTP / dFP > 0.2 DTI / (1 - 0.2 DTI).
  Break-even: 0.040 at DTI 0.192, 0.052 at 0.2477, 0.064 at 0.30, 0.068 at 0.3195.
  So at today's operating point a pixel needs only a ~5 % kernel-weighted return per unit of false-positive mass to pay for itself - cheap evidence is worth including,
  but a solid line of 1-px pixels is mostly paying for pixels that add no new credit.

## 2. Why dotting wins: credit per dot saturates at 3, cost does not
For a straight true line and dots on it with spacing s (kernel radius 3 px; computed exactly):

| spacing s (px) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| mean credit per true pixel | 1.000 | 0.833 | 0.778 | 0.667 | 0.600 | 0.500 | 0.375 |
| credit per dot (= credit x s) | 1.00 | 1.67 | 2.33 | 2.67 | 3.00 | 3.00 | 3.00 |

A dot costs 1 unit of FP mass when it is off-truth regardless of spacing. Going from a solid line to s = 3 keeps 78 % of the on-line credit for a third of the
mass. This is a pure emission-geometry effect: **no new geology**. The owner's group measured it twice: the same surface emitted solid (H25-ctx 0.1280) versus dotted
(H28 0.1839) gained +43.7 %; H19-5 (0.1922) thinned with `dot_thin(1.5)` gave 0.2477 (+28.9 %).
I regenerated the 0.2477 file from the H19-5 emission: `dot_thin(H19-5 minus catalogue, 1.5)` is **identical** (60,069 px) to the scored file (`scripts/build_submission27.py`, asserted on every build).

## 3. Reconstructed operating point at 0.2477 (*model*)
Assuming |G| = 12.63k and FP = 4.3|G| (about 54k px): weighted recall TP/|G| = 0.433.
The model of the sibling predicted 0.2548 for this file; the realised 0.2477 is 2.8 % lower, so the model is directionally right but optimistic.
Plausible range for FP/|G| 3.0-6.0 gives recall 0.37-0.52 and FP 38-76k px (`evidence/operating_point_model.json`, reproducible with `scripts/operating_point.py`).

| term of the denominator (units of |G|) | share |
|---|---|
| 0.2 TP  (0.087) | 5 % |
| 0.2 FP  (0.86) | 49 % |
| 0.8 |G|  (0.80) | 46 % |

## 4. What 0.3195 would require (*model*)
Starting from recall 0.433 / FP 54k px:

* same FP -> weighted recall **0.567** (+31 %), or
* same recall -> FP **29.5k px** (-46 %), or a mix on the line  0.2 FP/|G| = TP/(0.3195|G|) - 0.2 TP/|G| - 0.8.

Thinning alone cannot do this: the sibling's model puts the best thinning (d about 2.8) at +0.0095 over d1.5, i.e. about 0.257 realised. A 46 % FP cut at equal recall,
or +31 % recall at equal FP, needs **new information or a materially better detector** (e.g. a supervised detector on the 1 m DEM with geologist-labelled scarps - an access need, see 06).

## 6. Contrarian, grounded notes
* The best files in the family are all topography/scarp driven; point-wise AUCs of single channels are weak (best lidar `lappos_max` 0.58; magnetic and gravity values about 0.5 - faults are edges, not values; `evidence/channel_auc.json`).

## Addendum (2026-10-03, H32-1 closeout): corrected naive closure and what 0.3195 requires

The two-anchor emission model in this note was originally evaluated with a denominator that
double-counted `0.2·a` (`a/(a + 0.2·N/G + 0.8(1−a))`). The corrected naive closure, derived directly
from the official metric with the known-catalogue mask folded in and prediction-side credit set equal
to truth-side credit (`B = A`), is

    DTI = A / (A + 0.2·(N − A) + 0.8·(|G| − A)) = a / (0.8 + 0.2·N/|G|),   a = A/|G|.

`scripts/analyze_why_026_won.py` now implements the corrected closure and is the citable record
([`evidence/why_026_won.json`](../evidence/why_026_won.json)). Evaluated with the sibling's pinned
`credit_per_truth = 0.508792` and geometric retentions (0.853128 at d=1.5, 0.758876 at d=2.8) at the
sibling lattice calibration `|G| = 12,225.896 px` and the measured dot counts (121,131 / 60,069 /
44,090), it predicts:

| file | reported | corrected closure | residual |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| H19-5 solid | 0.1922 | 0.1829 | −0.0093 |
| dotted d=1.5 | 0.2477 | **0.2435** | −0.0042 |
| dotted d=2.8 | 0.2600 | **0.2538** | −0.0062 |

So the ordering and the magnitude of the packing-distance gain survive the correction: the d=2.8
family is predicted ~+0.010 above d=1.5, and the reported 0.2600 sits inside the sibling's published
interval [0.25003, 0.26133]. The residuals are systematic (≈ −0.005), i.e. the pinned constants were
themselves solved under the older closure and are not re-fit here; they are disclosed rather than
absorbed.

**What 0.3195 needs under the corrected closure.** At the current credit fraction
(`a = 0.508792 × 0.758876 = 0.3861`), 0.3195 requires `N ≈ 24,970` emitted pixels instead of 44,090
— the same credited fault mass concentrated in roughly half the dots. At the current dot count,
credit must rise from 0.386 to ~0.479 of the hidden truth (+24 %). Both routes are ranking problems
(which pixels to keep), not packing problems: `dot_thin`-style re-spacing alone cannot supply them.

### 7.1 Why and how `dotted-h19-5-d2-8-20261002-e56ea318af89-nan` achieved `0.2600`
1. **Exact construction (`data/dotted_h19_5_d2_8_nan.tif`, SHA-256 `91eae1ca42ec...`)**:
   - Takes the 6-expert scarp/geophysics ensemble ridge mask `H19-5` (`121,131` off-catalogue 1-px ridge cells, live `0.1922`), masks the `60,988` known USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue pixels, and applies priority-ordered Poisson-disk thinning (`dot_thin(min_dist=2.8)`), retaining **`44,090` binary pixels** (`0.8532%` of footprint) with minimum inter-dot Euclidean distance $\ge \sqrt{8} \approx 2.828\text{ px}$ ($283\text{ m}$).
2. **Why `d=2.8` beat `d=1.5` (`0.2477` -> `0.2600`, `+0.0123` DTI) AND beat the 2D geometric model (`0.2550` -> `0.2600`, `+0.0050` DTI)**:
   - Under the official DTI kernel $k(d) = \max(0, 1 - d/3\text{ px})$, two dots separated by $s \in [2.828, 3.0]\text{ px}$ along a 1D fault crest have their midpoint at $d = s/2 \in [1.414, 1.50]\text{ px}$, where every intermediate truth pixel still receives $k(d) \in [0.50, 0.529]$ credit!
   - Meanwhile, the false-positive denominator penalty drops by $0.2 \times \Delta N = 0.2 \times (60,069 - 44,090) = 3,195.8$ denominator units, and the kernel crowding factor $\rho = \text{MP}_w / \text{TP}_w$ drops from `2.460` (solid) $\to$ `1.429` (`d=1.5`) $\to$ **`1.179`** (`d=2.8`).
   - Crucially, the 2D isotropic uniform-truth retention formula (`evidence/budget_optimum.json`) predicted `c(d=2.8)/c(d=1.5) = 0.8896` (implying `0.2550` live), whereas the **actual live credit retention is `4,791.0 / 5,286.1 = 0.9063` (`+1.88%` higher than 2D isotropic retention)**! Because hidden truth $G$ is a **1D curvilinear fault network colinear with the `H19-5` ridge crest**, thinning along a 1D curve at $d=2.8\text{ px}$ preserves `90.63%` of on-crest kernel credit while cutting dot count by `26.60%`!

### 7.2 Why `topo-gap-closure-t-v2-on-d1-5` (`5512495c6bd1`) scored `0.2449` (`-0.0028` vs `0.2477`)
- `5512495c6bd1` added `1,259` non-redundant `T-v2` straight-line gap-closure dots across `345` links (`1–4 km` gaps between published Qfaults tips) onto `d=1.5` (`60,069` $\to$ `61,328` px).
- Inverting `0.2449` vs `0.2477` shows `credit_TPw` rose from `5,286.13 px` to `5,288.78 px` — **only `+2.65 px` of true credit across `1,259` added dots (`e_add = 0.00210` credit/dot, `23.5x` below the `0.0495` break-even threshold)**!
- **Geological explanation**: When a regional compiler leaves a `1–4 km` gap inside a named fault zone in the NBMG compilation, a catalogue-internal hide-and-recover test artificially splits continuous polylines (`230/345` `T-v2` links were same-FID sub-parts) and rewards straight-line interpolation (`e_oof = 0.2251`). In contrast, the competition's hidden test set $G$ consists of **newly mapped faults omitted from the compilation**, not un-digitised `1–4 km` straight segments inside already-mapped Qfaults polylines. Thus `T-v2` must be excluded from all future submissions.

### 7.3 Can we generate a submission that scores higher than `0.2600`?
- **Yes — to `~0.266–0.270` directly from the existing `H19-5` detector via tip- & Euler-protected mid-segment de-jittering (`c3aeda1d31a3`, `31e35eee884e`) or solo `H27-4` (`8acb75e1f2cc`) on `d=2.8` (without `T-v2`)**:
  - In `dotted-h19-5-d2-8` (`44,090` px, `0.2600`), **`3,891` dots (`8.83%` of the entire submission)** sit at $d_{\text{cat}} = 1.0\text{ px}$ ($100\text{ m}$) immediately adjacent to masked known catalogue faults.
  - Partitioning those `3,891` $d_{\text{cat}}=1\text{ px}$ dots into **`2,434` mid-segment lateral flank-shadow dots** ($d_{\text{end}} > 300\text{ m}$, $\text{cat\_nbrs} \ge 2$, $d_{\text{Euler}} > 300\text{ m}$) and **`1,457` protected tip-continuation or shallow Euler $N=0$ depth-cluster dots** ($d_{\text{end}} \le 300\text{ m}$ or $d_{\text{Euler}} \le 300\text{ m}$) and validating on the 4-fold spatially-blocked OOF holdout (seeds `180–189`, `evidence/h32_1_holdout.json`) proves that mid-segment flank-shadow dots carry `0.0040` credit/FP (`2.29x` lower than protected tip/Euler dots at `0.0092` credit/FP, and `13.7x` below the `0.0549` live break-even at `0.2600`).
  - Removing the `2,434` mid-segment flank-shadow dots from `d=2.8` (`gems28-h32-1-tip-euler-dejitter-d2-8-20261003-c3aeda1d31a3-nan.tif`, `41,656` px) improves 4-fold spatial-CV holdout in **`10/10` seeds and `4/4` folds (`+0.001272` post-thinning, `+0.001399` pre-thinning)** and projects to **`0.2665` (`+0.0065` over `0.2600`)** on the live-anchored hybrid forward model (or **`0.2701`** for the unprotected solo `H27-4` `d=2.8` prune `8acb75e1f2cc`, `40,199` px).
- **Reaching `0.3195` (`DARD` #1)**: requires raising far-field recall $\text{TP}_w / |G|$ from `0.392` (`4,791` px) to `~0.52+` (`~6,350+` px) at $\sim 40\text{k}$ dots, which cannot be achieved by post-processing `H19-5` alone and requires a dedicated 2D spatial CNN/U-Net (`FaultSEG` / `siUNET` architecture per Hermant et al. 2025) trained with shallow-over-deep magnetic gradient de-screening (`H32-2`) and gravity-bench decoupling (`H32-3`).

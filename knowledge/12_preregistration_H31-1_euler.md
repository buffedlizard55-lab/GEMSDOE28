# Preregistration H31-1 — depth-labeled magnetic Euler solutions and shallow clusters

**This version is committed before any H31 classifier fit or holdout.** The initial label-free Euler feature audit records the same earlier protocol bytes, but the checkout's Git history cannot establish whether those bytes were committed before that earlier solve; the chronology limitation is disclosed in `evidence/h31_1_prereg_history_audit.json`. This protocol is the current proposal for the Euler arm. It is not an outcome, does not authorize a weekly upload, and may not be tuned after seeds 160–169 are inspected. The implementation and synthetic tests may be reviewed before the screen; no classifier fit or holdout may run until this exact file is committed.

## Objective and falsifiable geological hypothesis

**Hypothesis.** Spatial clusters of depth-labeled magnetic-source solutions from Euler deconvolution of the TMI field, when they are shallow and lie near independently extracted magnetic lineament ridges, may identify fault-related contacts or source boundaries that are absent from the published Quaternary-fault catalogue. The source locations must come from the Euler least-squares solutions and their clusters—not from maxima of a horizontal-gradient, vertical-gradient, or edge raster. Gradient ridges are used only as a post-solution alignment filter and as a reported comparison layer.

**Target.** Fault-location pixels. This is not a geothermal-favorability, spring-proximity, or lithology-boundary classifier. A magnetic source edge is not proof of a fault: intrusive contacts, volcanic units, remanent magnetization, survey artifacts and unrelated susceptibility boundaries are plausible confounders.

**How this differs from prior work in this checkout.** H28-1 added multiscale magnetic/gravity edge-coherence covariates; earlier detectors also include magnetic derivatives as scalar inputs and apply non-maximum suppression to detector probabilities. No checked-in experiment here solves Euler's four unknowns, records source depth/coordinates, or clusters those source solutions. The proposed method converts a magnetic field into depth-labeled source hypotheses before learning; it does not reinterpret a gradient peak as a source location. Novelty beyond this checkout and public prior art is not claimed.

## Frozen transform and Euler solver

### Derivative field

- Use band 14 `tmi` only. Verify band number/name, dtype, CRS, affine transform, template hash and template mask. No resampling and no labels in this transform.
- Form east and north horizontal derivatives by centered finite differences on the 100 m grid; preserve the source raster's field units per grid cell. Those units are not authenticated by the mirror's tags, so no physical-unit calibration is claimed. Positive north is `-dT/drow`.
- Form the downward vertical derivative as `|k| T` on overlapping **128 × 128** tiles, with 100 m cell size and a Tukey taper (alpha 0.25); retain only each tile's central **64 × 64** core, with tile/core stride **64**. Fourier wavenumber is angular (`2π f`) and the derivative is multiplied by 100 m so all components remain in the source field's units per grid cell. Positive depth/down is positive. Nearest-valid filling is permitted only to supply FFT context for a tile with at least 99.9% valid cells; only cells valid in the sample footprint are retained. Tiles below that validity threshold or cores with any invalid cell provide no solve. This deterministic local spectral derivative is a model choice, not an organizer-provided product; edge and tile effects are a material limitation. Because `TMI` and all derivatives use the same band, a constant multiplicative unit conversion cancels from the Euler position/depth solution; that invariance does not supply missing physical-unit provenance.
- Use the same local coordinate convention for every fit: x east, y north, z positive down; x/y are expressed in 100 m cells relative to the fixed grid center. `TMI`, gradients and coordinates are never normalized using labels or holdout outcomes.

### Window solve and structural index

- Primary structural index **SI = 0**, moving window 10 × 10 cells (1 km × 1 km), start stride 4 cells (400 m). This follows Reid et al.'s SI-dependent source-geometry framework; SI 0 is an approximation for a contact with effectively infinite depth extent, not a generic fault model. Real faults are finite, dipping, segmented, and may require higher SI. Euler deconvolution estimates source position/depth and does **not** estimate fault dip.
- For each window, solve the least-squares Euler system for the four unknowns `(x_source, y_source, z_source, A)` with 100 equations and SI 0. The fourth unknown is the SI-0 base-level/offset term; do not substitute an unreported `B` or silently drop it. Use column-scaled least squares. Record singular values, scaled condition number, residual RMS, depth standard error and source/window coordinates.
- Run **SI = 1 and SI = 2** over the exact same windows only as frozen structural-index sensitivity analyses, not as alternative features from which the best-looking result may be selected. For SI > 0 use the standard `SI × T` term and fit the corresponding regional/base-level offset. Report their solution counts, depth distributions and spatial stability separately. SI results may not be tuned against holdout labels.
- Accept an individual solution only when: all 100 cells have valid TMI and all three derivatives; the column-scaled condition number is ≤ 1e5; the least-squares relative depth standard error is ≤ 0.15; the positive-down SI-0 depth is 100–3,000 m; and the source x/y lies within the 10 × 10 window plus a 2-cell tolerance. Apply the same numeric diagnostics to SI 1/2. The 15% criterion is a predeclared empirical filter motivated by Reid et al.'s examples, not a universal confidence guarantee.
- These thresholds deliberately reject some possible sources. We will report counts before and after each filter, not describe rejected solutions as evidence against a fault.

### Clusters, lineament alignment, and model features

- Derive a candidate lineament mask from the **horizontal-gradient magnitude computed from this same TMI field**, using the existing deterministic `ridge_nms(sigma=1)` implementation. It is solely an alignment test; it does not set source x/y positions. Retain only accepted SI-0 solutions whose independently solved source coordinates are within 200 m (2 cells) of this mask, then cluster those aligned solutions with DBSCAN, `eps=600 m`, `min_samples=4`. Retain a cluster only if median depth is 100–3,000 m and depth MAD ≤ 500 m. A cluster's location is the median Euler `(x,y)` solution coordinate; its depth label is the median positive-down SI-0 depth. These rules are frozen before any label holdout.
- Append exactly three deterministic, label-free rasters to the existing 38-column OOF input (32 existing features + six H28-1 potential-field edge features), using only members of retained clusters: (1) Gaussian-smoothed (`sigma=2 cells`, constant-zero boundary) count, transformed as `clip(log1p(count)/log1p(5),0,1)`; (2) Gaussian-smoothed sum of per-solution shallow weights `clip(1-depth/3000m,0,1)`, transformed by the same `clip(log1p(sum)/log1p(5),0,1)`; (3) local member-weighted cluster-median depth divided by 3, calculated from Gaussian-smoothed depth-km numerator / Gaussian-smoothed member count. Invalid and outside-footprint cells are zero in these three append-only features. No Euler solutions are inserted as submission dots; no candidate raster is built before the holdout.
- Report raw SI-0 solution points, clusters and depth labels in a separate research artifact; do not publish this as ground-truth geology. Report the percentage of the footprint for which valid Euler windows/cores exist so coverage loss is visible.

## Frozen gates and promotion rule

**Pre-fit data-sufficiency stop:** before fitting either OOF model, the label-free feature build must cover at least 50% of valid in-footprint magnetic cells with valid vertical derivatives, retain at least 100 accepted SI-0 Euler solutions, and produce at least 10 depth-coherent lineament-aligned SI-0 clusters. If any threshold fails, stop without fitting, record the counts, and mark H31-1 not testable on this mirror; do not change the thresholds after seeing them.

Each stage (screen and, if earned, confirmation) passes only if all hold:

1. mean paired candidate-minus-control DTI ≥ **+0.0010** over 40 paired cells;
2. mean gain is positive in at least **3 of 4** folds;
3. gain is positive in at least **8 of 10** seed means;
4. all input/transform/window masks, training buffers, label exclusion, probabilities, candidate spacing, grid and catalogue-overlap checks pass; and
5. the run has no unreported transform/code/hash changes.

A failed stage rejects H31-1 for submission; no seed reuse or retuning. Passing both stages may justify making a uniquely content-addressed, unscored full-map **research** candidate. It still does not establish transfer to organizer labels, a leaderboard score, or a win. A weekly-slot promotion additionally requires the separate implementation/source review, bug/leakage review, full exact-file audit against the current template, and a reproducible holdout artifact; no automatic upload/monitoring is allowed. The human owner retains manual submission control.

## H35-1 — Hydrothermal upflow conjunction ADD (RUNS THIS SESSION, seeds 230–239)

- **Layers (all held locally, hash-pinned or label-free builds).**
  1. `data/gdr_wellspring_in_footprint.csv` — 27,092 INGENIOUS well/spring records (OpenEI GDR
     #1391 mirror); columns `temp_c`, `geothermquartz_c`, grid `row`/`col`.
  2. `data/geodawn_extensions_u8.tif` bands 1 (`ThK`) and 3 (`UTh`) — airborne radiometric ratios
     (USGS GeoDAWN, Glen et al. 2024, ScienceBase `657e1d85d34e23d3533209f7` mirror).
  3. Sub-threshold OOF ridge from the seed's masked-label detector (same instrument as LOSFO).
  4. `evidence/h31_1_euler_clusters.csv` — 6,309 SI=0 Euler depth clusters (bonus leg only).
- **Physical signature.** Active fault-hosted upflow corrodes the very scarp the catalogue needs:
  argillic/sericitic alteration weakens wall rock, hot fluids erase brittle relief, and the fault
  enters no Quaternary scarp map — while (i) wells/springs stay hot (temp_c ≥ 60 °C or quartz
  geothermometer ≥ 130 °C), (ii) potassium metasomatism depletes Th/K and elevates U/Th in a halo
  around the conduit, (iii) the structure still ridges in the 32-band geophysical surface
  (below the emission cut), and (iv) a shallow Euler contact solution corroborates the feeder at
  depth. Each leg alone is weak; the four-way conjunction is the signature. Frozen rule:
  eligible = sub-threshold ridge ≥ 300 m from known catalogue AND within 1 km of a
  200 m-deduped hot cell; score = ridge_prob × (1 + 0.5·alteration + 0.5·Euler);
  budget = 2% of base dots (~880 full-map), dot-thinned at 2.8 px with ≥ 2.8 px clearance from
  base dots. Full frozen detail: `knowledge/24_preregistration_H35-1.md`.
- **Why it catches catalogue-missing faults.** The Qfaults compilation is a surface-rupture
  compilation: a fault with no preserved scarp is not in it by construction. Hydrothermal
  conduits (Brady's, Desert Peak, Dixie Valley, Salt Wells systems and their undiscovered
  analogues) are exactly the faults whose scarps are destroyed by their own fluids while their
  heat and alteration halos survive. 403 of 491 deduped hot cells sit ≥ 300 m from any
  catalogued pixel — the thermal channel lives in the far field where the submission must go.
- **Difference from repo.** H32-4 (ranked Session 8, never preregistered) proposed the same
  *layers* as a corridor screen; H35-1 reframes it as a capped, scored, LOSFO-gated **addition**
  with a live-threshold gate, a no-thermal direction control, and frozen leakage guards
  (per-cell distances recomputed vs the buffered known set — the CSV's full-catalogue distance
  column is never touched). GEMSDOE26's dilcond (live-refuted 0.1223) used only
  `cond_surf` × dilatation with no well, geothermometer, radiometric, or depth leg.
  H33-3/H35-2 is the same physical family with purpose-built residual data; H35-1 gates the
  family first with proxies, so a fail here lowers H35-2's prior before its seeds are spent.
- **Validation.** Preregistered LOSFO far-field gate on fresh seeds 230–239 (40 cells):
  added-dot marginal credit/dot must clear τ_live = 0.05485 in ≥ 8/10 seeds and ≥ 3/4 folds,
  beat the no-thermal control pooled, and cover ≥ 50% of cells. **No slot before PASS; on FAIL
  no TIFF, no retune, no fresh-decade rerun.**

## H35-2 (=H33-3) — Heat-flow residual × 2 m probe thermal conjunction ADD

- **Layers.** USGS DeAngelo et al. 2022 heat-flow release, DOI
  [10.5066/P9BZPVUC](https://doi.org/10.5066/P9BZPVUC), ScienceBase item
  [6297d2fad34ec53d276c5b28](https://www.sciencebase.gov/catalog/item/6297d2fad34ec53d276c5b28):
  the 7,279-well point coverage with `hf_resid`/`hfqc_resid`/`hftc_resid` residual attributes
  (departure from de-convected LOESS background — convective influence explicitly removed, so
  residuals mark exactly the hydrothermal upflow the compilation ignores) × GDR #1391 2 m
  temperature probes (5,151 in footprint per the runner byte-verification) × candidate
  lineaments. Adopted unchanged from `knowledge/18` (H33-3); not duplicated here.
- **Why it catches catalogue-missing faults.** Same family as H35-1, but the residual attribute
  is the purpose-built quantity: measured minus background-conductive heat flow, with
  convectively influenced outliers de-weighted by the 2D-LOESS trend fit. A buried,
  alluvium-mantled fault with no scarp still vents heat past the 2 m probes.
- **Difference from repo / H35-1.** No arm has used heat-flow data of any kind. Vs H35-1 it
  trades proxy breadth (27k wells/springs + alteration) for quantity quality (a designed
  residual + independent probe confirmation); the two compose (H35-1's hot cells cross-check
  H35-2's residual outliers) rather than compete.
- **Obtainability (checked 2026-10-03, this session).** Pin-verified bytes: `sb_heat_flow_zip`
  130,154,244 B, SHA-256 `e7fd62c6…` (`registry/external_pins.json`, runner-recorded). Zip
  contents confirmed *before* the run from the official FGDC metadata XML (read this session):
  `USGS_gbHeatFlowWells_wEstimates.shp` (7,279 entity points, Albers NAD83, residual attribute
  domains documented, e.g. `hf_resid` −91.4…+2957.9 mW/m²) + three
  `USGS_gbHeatFlowMap_{equalWts,qcWts,tcWts}.tif` grids. `DERIVED_SPECS` gained the
  `sb_heat_flow_zip → sb_heat_flow_in_footprint` spec this session with a point-coverage
  end-to-end regression test, so **merging this session's PR lands the derived clip**; the
  H35-2 preregistration (next session) requires the derived schema to list the residual fields
  before any seed is spent. The 2 m-probe leg still needs GDR-derived rasterization (remaining
  work: extend the bridge to GDR zips or dispatch an availability-only=false run — the agent
  token cannot dispatch).

## H35-3 (=H33-4) — Drainage-network neotectonics from 1 m DEMs ADD

- **Layers.** Competition `dem_links.json` (716 tiles, verbatim keys) → USGS 3DEP 1 m DEMs (free,
  official, public S3 bucket `prd-tnm.s3.amazonaws.com/StagedProducts/Elevation/1m/Projects/`);
  derived flow-direction/accumulation networks; local `lidar_scarp_features_u8.tif` for
  contrast. Adopted from `knowledge/18` (H33-4): channel offsets/deflections, beheaded streams,
  aligned knickpoint trains, wind gaps along candidate lineaments.
- **Why it catches catalogue-missing faults.** Channels integrate the youngest deformation:
  alluvial-basin faults erase scarps fastest but keep offsetting active channels, so drainage
  response is the recorder the Quaternary-scarp compilation misses most systematically.

  roughness, openness); no arm has touched flow-network topology.

## H35-4 — Buried range-front gravity-bench ADD (NEW)

- **Layers (all held locally).** `training_features.tif` band 21 (`grav_hg`), band 19 (`isograv`),
  band 4 (`det_local_relief`); `lidar_scarp_features_u8.tif` (`lidar_step_max`, `lidar_rough50`);
  band 15 (`tmi_hg`); sub-threshold OOF ridge; catalogue distance fields.
- **Physical signature.** A range-front normal fault buried under a Holocene fanglomerate apron
  juxtaposes dense Paleozoic/Mesozoic basement (2,650–2,750 kg/m³) against unconsolidated basin
  fill (2,000–2,200 kg/m³) → a strong `grav_hg` inflection along a low-roughness alluvial-fan
  break-in-slope — with NO crisp 1 m LiDAR scarp and no magnetic edge. Emit capped dots where
  high-`grav_hg` + moderate-`det_local_relief` + low-`lidar_rough50` coincide on far-field
  sub-threshold ridge, explicitly excluding contour-following volcanic mesa rims (high
  `lidar_step_max` + high `tmi_hg` + near-zero `grav_hg`, the H19-5 false-positive mode).
- **Why it catches catalogue-missing faults.** Pediment-front and range-front faults degraded by
  fanglomerate progradation lose the crisp scarp the USGS compilation keys on, while the
  basement density step survives burial untouched. Gravity sees through the apron; the mapper's
  aerial photo does not.
- **Difference from repo.** H32-3 specified the same layers as *suppression* (prune-class) and
  is closed unrun under the pruning-exhausted rule. H35-4 is its ADD dual: emit buried-fault
  candidates instead of suppressing mesa rims — different gate (LOSFO + τ_live), different
  budget (capped addition), different failure mode. H28-1 computed magnetic-gravity orientation
  coherence globally without the mesa-vs-bench decoupling.
- **Validation (next, if H35-1 leaves seed budget pressure acceptable).** Fresh decade 240–249,
  same LOSFO + τ_live gate shape as H35-1 with a no-gravity direction control. Preregister
  before touching.

## H35-5 (=H33-5) — Phase-2 discovery budget (bounded ADD policy)

- **Rule basis (official, re-verified by prior sessions).** Official rules
  ([PDF 96647](https://docs.nlr.gov/docs/fy26osti/96647.pdf)): Phase 2 re-scores *the same
  submission* against an expert-expanded label set built by cross-referencing every team's
  submission; "predictions that helped experts identify previously-unmapped faults can score
  higher here than in the Initial Prize Round" (DrivenData problem page). Metric asymmetry
  (α=0.2 FP vs β=0.8 FN) makes false positives cheap. Adopted from `knowledge/18` (H33-5).
- **Policy.** A small bounded budget (≤ ~600–900 px, ≈ the H35-1 2% scale) of maximally
  corroborated novel segments — dots with ≥ 3 independent indicators (LiDAR scarp + shallow
  Euler cluster + thermal/gravity conjunction) and > 300 m from any catalogue pixel. Phase-1
  worst-case cost at 880 zero-credit dots: denominator +176 units ≈ **−0.0025 DTI** (analytic
  bound at the 0.2600 operating point) — while creating asymmetric Phase-2 upside if experts
  confirm any of them.
- **Why last.** It is the only arm aimed at the Phase-2 rescoring rule, and the only structural
  path to leapfrog a leaderboard cluster plausibly sharing the H19-5-family surface — but its
  core claim is not holdout-testable on any catalogue-derived truth, so it composes *with* a
  passing ADD arm rather than leading. If H35-1 passes LOSFO, its top-conjunction dots ARE the
  discovery budget's first allocation.

Reid, Allsop, Granser, Millett & Somerton (1990), Magnetic interpretation in three dimensions using Euler deconvolution

Geophysics / SEG; author-hosted copy · primary-method-literature · accessed 2026-10-03

Verification status: Full author-hosted PDF fetched and read through page service on 2026-10-03; DOI resolves the primary article.

Use: H31-1 preregistration: structural-index-dependent Euler source solutions, depth uncertainty, window-size tradeoffs, solution clustering and geological caveats.

Evidence/limitation: The paper describes SI-dependent source clustering and the empirical nature of solution rejection thresholds. SI 0 approximates contacts with effectively infinite depth extent; finite/complex faults may require higher indices. Incorrect SI can bias depth. Multiple sources, poor gridding, noise and gradients can yield rejected or spurious solutions; window size trades shallow resolution against representation of deeper sources. Euler supplies source position/depth estimates, not fault dip.

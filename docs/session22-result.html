Catalogue-flank blur dots (100–200 m from mapped faults) earn only ~0.0034 credit/dot (17× below τ); deleting 6,436 of them removed ~1,280 denominator FPw mass while sacrificing ≈22 TPw. Poisson-disk thinning at 2.8 px works identically: contiguous 1-px ridges pay 3× FPw inside each 300 m disk while maxx k(‖x−g‖) is already saturated at ≥0.53 by 2.8-px spaced dots.

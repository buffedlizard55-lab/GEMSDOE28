Session 10 — what beating 0.3195 costs, in pixels of credit

The gap is a detection gap, not a budget gap. scripts/reachability_frontier.py (evidence/reachability_frontier.json) inverts the official metric. The closed form DTI = TPw / (0.2·TPw·(1−ρ) + 0.2·N + 0.8·|G|) is checked numerically against metric.dti_binary on 23 synthetic grids before use — max absolute residual 2.22e-16, and the substitution FPw = N − MPw holds in every case. At the calibrated |G| = 12,225 px, the best owner-anchored submission earns 4,791 px of credit (39.2% of |G|) at N = 44,090. Reaching 0.3195 at that same budget needs 5,942 px — a gap of +1,151 px, 24.0% more credit than the entire 0.2600 submission captures.

Why thinning cannot buy it

The programme has one exactly-controlled live measurement of marginal efficiency: thinning the same ridge from d=1.5 (60,069 px, 0.2477) to d=2.8 (44,090 px, 0.2600) removed 15,979 dots and lost 495.1 px of credit — 0.03098 credit/px, below the break-even 0.05485 at 0.2600. A dot earning that little does not clear the 0.2·target charge, so no quantity of dots at the current marginal efficiency reaches 0.3195.

Pruning arms are still worth taking — they are free and compose — but each is worth ~0.001–0.010, one to two orders of magnitude short.

What would reach it

New dots that land on genuinely unmapped fault traces, by the credit each earns:

Session 10 — a far-field holdout that can finally gate addition arms

Why it was needed: the standing holdout hides catalogue components interleaved with the known catalogue, so 100% of its hidden truth lies at distance 0 from the published catalogue (evidence/arm_habitat_decomposition.json: habitat-A truth = 0 of 120,983 px). A shippable file may place no pixel on a catalogue cell, so that protocol contains no truth in the only habitat a submission can occupy — it cannot validate any arm that proposes dots where nothing is catalogued, which §above says is the only class that can reach 0.3195.

Structural-index instability

SI-1 retains 5,666 clusters; 41.0% of SI-0 centroids match within 600 m and median nearest-centroid distance is 855 m.

SI-2 retains 4,661 clusters; 30.2% match within 600 m and median distance is 1154 m. SI=1/2 are descriptive sensitivity checks, not alternative indices to select after seeing a favorable holdout.

SI=0 approximates an idealized contact with effectively infinite depth extent. Real faults may be finite, dipping, intersecting or have mixed geometry and may require higher indices. Euler does not estimate dip; the structural index is a geological model choice, not an automatically “correct” value. Candidate gradient ridges are used only to test alignment with Euler-derived source solutions, never as the inferred source locations.

Official USGS GeoDAWN metadata reports nominal magnetic flight-line spacing of 200 m in Area 1 and 400 m in Area 2, with variable terrain clearance. The 100 m output grid is not independent 100 m survey resolution. The exact pinned mirror is not organizer-authenticated, and derivative units/conventions have not been calibrated against survey units.

Rank 1 · H32-2

Shallow-over-deep magnetic gradient de-screening (intrusive-pluton margin suppression)

UNTRIED.

Layers:

  • training_features.tif band 15 (tmi_hg)
  • training_features.tif band 17 (tmi_rtp)
  • geodawn_extensions_u8.tif band 4 (TMI_up150, 150 m upward-continued TMI)
  • training_features.tif band 18 (mag_sed_thick_km)
  • training_features.tif band 5 (depth_to_base_surf)

Physical signature: Upward-continuation attenuation ratio R_shallow = |nabla_H(TMI)| / (|nabla_H(TMI_up150)| + eps) paired with shallow basement/magnetic-sediment thickness. Shallow brittle fault contacts (z_0 <= 300 m) decay rapidly under 150 m upward continuation (high R_shallow), whereas deep Cenozoic pluton margins and caldera ring complexes (z_0 >= 1.5-3 km) decay slowly (low R_shallow ~ 1) while still generating strong raw tmi_hg ridges.

Why it might find faults missing from the catalogue: Concealed shallow intrabasinal fault contacts beneath thin alluvial cover (mag_sed_thick_km < 0.8 km) lack LiDAR scarps and are omitted from the Quaternary scarp-biased compilation, whereas deep intrusive-body boundaries in ranges produce false-positive far-field magnetic ridges that are not brittle faults.

Different from prior work: TMI_up150 in geodawn_extensions_u8.tif was only appended as an un-ratioed uint8 column in the failed H27-12 +rad/+all detector screen; its horizontal-gradient attenuation ratio against tmi_hg has never been computed or used to de-screen far-field H19-5 ridges.

Confounders: Shallow subhorizontal volcanic flow edges can also exhibit high upward-continuation decay; requires cross-checking against topographic contour parallelism.

Planning-only ΔDTI range: 0.001 to +0.003. This is an uncertain prior, not an observed holdout result or leaderboard prediction.

Cost: Low (~25 min implementation + 4-fold spatial-CV holdout; all inputs restored and hash-verified locally).

Data/access gate: All bands present locally in data/training_features.tif and data/geodawn_extensions_u8.tif (USGS GeoDAWN ScienceBase item 657e1d85d34e23d3533209f7).

Validation: Preregister R_shallow far-field ridge swap/filter on d=2.8 before running on unused seeds 190-199.

Rank 2 · H32-3

Gravity-gradient bench inflection vs basalt-capped mesa topographic decoupling

UNTRIED.

Layers:

  • training_features.tif band 21 (grav_hg)
  • training_features.tif band 19 (isograv)
  • training_features.tif band 4 (det_local_relief)
  • lidar_scarp_features_u8.tif band 2 (lidar_step_max) and band 5 (lidar_rough50)
  • training_features.tif band 15 (tmi_hg)

Physical signature: Co-aligned local maxima of horizontal gravity gradient (grav_hg) and low-roughness alluvial-fan break-in-slope (det_local_relief, lidar_step_max), contrasted against high-tmi_hg, zero-grav_hg erosional volcanic mesa rims that follow elevation contours.

Why it might find faults missing from the catalogue: Pediment-front and older range-front normal faults buried by Holocene fanglomerate aprons lack fresh 1 m LiDAR scarps and are omitted from USGS Qfaults, but juxtapose dense Paleozoic/Mesozoic basement (2,650-2,750 kg/m3) against unconsolidated basin fill (2,000-2,200 kg/m3), creating a persistent grav_hg inflection.

Different from prior work: H28-1 tested magnetic-gravity gradient orientation coherence across all pixels without distinguishing erosional volcanic rims from basement-basin density steps; H27-13 tested oriented LiDAR line integrals without gravity or magnetic decoupling.

Confounders: 2 km regional gravity station spacing limits spatial precision of grav_hg to +-300-500 m; paleochannel incised terraces can mimic low-relief benches.

Planning-only ΔDTI range: 0.001 to +0.002. This is an uncertain prior, not an observed holdout result or leaderboard prediction.

Cost: Low-medium (~35 min implementation + 4-fold spatial-CV holdout; all bands restored locally).

Data/access gate: All bands present locally in data/training_features.tif and data/lidar_scarp_features_u8.tif.

Validation: Preregister mesa-rim suppression and range-front gravity-bench retention on unused seeds before holdout execution.

Rank 3 · H32-4

Quality-screened hydrothermal geothermometer and K/Th-U/Th alteration halos along sub-scarp corridors

UNTRIED.

Layers:

  • data/gdr_wellspring_in_footprint.csv (27,092 INGENIOUS well/spring records)
  • data/geodawn_extensions_u8.tif (Th/K, U/K, U/Th airborne radiometric ratios)
  • training_features.tif band 9 (cond_surf) and band 8 (geod_dilaterate)

Physical signature: Anisotropic fault-strike-aligned hydrothermal alteration corridors coupling quality-screened high-temperature wells/springs (tempc >= 60 C or gt_qtz >= 130 C, de-duplicated to 200 m cells) with potassium-metasomatic depletion of Th/K (low Th/K, elevated U/Th) along sub-threshold geodetector ridges.

Why it might find faults missing from the catalogue: Permeable hydrothermal fault conduits in active geothermal districts (e.g., Brady's, Desert Peak, Dixie Valley, Salt Wells) undergo argillic/sericitic alteration that mechanically softens surface rocks and erases brittle scarps, causing omission from Quaternary scarp maps while leaving potassium/uranium radiometric halos and thermal outflows.

Different from prior work: H27-12 tested isotropic Euclidean distances to all unscreened wells/springs and raw radiometric bands independently and found near-zero PR-AUC gain (+0.0005 to +0.0007). H26 dilcond-oof-v1 (live 0.1223) used geod_dilaterate x cond_surf alone without hydrothermal points or radiometric K/Th alteration ratios.

Confounders: Playa evaporites and felsic tuffs produce non-fault radiometric anomalies; lateral outflow plumes can displace thermal springs 0.5-1.5 km down-gradient from the upflow fault.

Planning-only ΔDTI range: 0.000 to +0.002. This is an uncertain prior, not an observed holdout result or leaderboard prediction.

Cost: Medium (~45 min implementation + fold-safe spatial encoding; all data restored locally).

Data/access gate: All files present and hash-verified locally in data/gdr_wellspring_in_footprint.csv and data/geodawn_extensions_u8.tif (GDR #1391 & USGS GeoDAWN).

Validation: Preregister quality-screened hydrothermal + Th/K alteration corridor boost on unused seeds before holdout execution.

  • SI-0 primary: 118,089 accepted source solutions; 76,915 aligned to gradient ridges within 200 m; 6,309 retained depth-coherent clusters.

Structural-index instability

SI-1 retains 5,666 clusters; 41.0% of SI-0 centroids match within 600 m and median nearest-centroid distance is 855 m.

SI-2 retains 4,661 clusters; 30.2% match within 600 m and median distance is 1154 m. SI=1/2 are descriptive sensitivity checks, not alternative indices to select after seeing a favorable holdout.

SI=0 approximates an idealized contact with effectively infinite depth extent. Real faults may be finite, dipping, intersecting or have mixed geometry and may require higher indices. Euler does not estimate dip; the structural index is a geological model choice, not an automatically “correct” value. Candidate gradient ridges are used only to test alignment with Euler-derived source solutions, never as the inferred source locations.

Official USGS GeoDAWN metadata reports nominal magnetic flight-line spacing of 200 m in Area 1 and 400 m in Area 2, with variable terrain clearance. The 100 m output grid is not independent 100 m survey resolution. The exact pinned mirror is not organizer-authenticated, and derivative units/conventions have not been calibrated against survey units.

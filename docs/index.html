PhD analysis · 24-submission live-score inversion

Why dotted-h19-5-d2-8 scored 0.2600, why T-v2 scored 0.2449, and how to beat 0.2600.

Why e56ea318af89 (d=2.8) achieved 0.2600

Priority-ordered Poisson-disk thinning of the 6-expert scarp/geophysics ridge surface H19-5 (121,131 px, live 0.1922) at min_dist=2.8 px emits 44,090 off-catalogue dots (0.8532% footprint). Under the 300 m (3 px) linear tolerance kernel, dots spaced at 2.83–3.0 px along a 1D fault crest overlap at 1.41–1.50 px midpoints with k ≥ 0.50, retaining 90.63% of d=1.5 live credit (4,791.0 vs 5,286.1 px at |G| = 12,226 px) while cutting 15,979 redundant dots (−26.60% FP mass) and reducing kernel crowding ρ from 1.429 to 1.179. Because true faults are 1D curves along ridge crests rather than 2D isotropic sheets, live retention beat the 2D uniform-truth prediction (0.2550) by +0.0050. Open 24-submission inversion →

Why 5512495c6bd1 (T-v2) scored 0.2449 & how we beat 0.2600

Adding 1,259 straight-line T-v2 gap-closure dots onto d=1.5 (60,069 → 61,328 px) earned only +2.65 px of live credit (0.00210 credit/dot vs 0.0495 break-even), lowering live DTI by −0.0028; 26GEMSDOE dilcond-oof-v1 (47629f496133) scored 0.1223. Conversely, 3,891 dots (8.83%) of the 0.2600 d=2.8 file sit at dcat = 100 m beside masked known faults due to 100–400 m USGS-vs-LiDAR scarp offsets (Hermant et al., 2025). Pruning the 2,434 mid-segment flank-shadow dots while protecting fault tips and shallow SI-0 Euler depth clusters (H32-1, c3aeda1d31a3, 41,656 px) projects to 0.2665; solo H27-4 8acb75e1f2cc (40,199 px) to 0.2701. H36-1 supersedes both: the H34 live-anchored ladder says the optimal packing rung is 3.0, not the shipped 2.828, and the rung ladder is not nested — raising the rung re-seeds the greedy Poisson-disk packing and changes 24,091 px of the 44,090-px emission, it does not merely delete 2,757 dots. Re-packing to rung 3.0 and then applying the H27-4 blind flank prune (37,660 px) projects to 0.2717–0.2727 live, and it passed a fresh 10-seed gate at +0.002599 ΔDTI while a coin-flip deletion to the same dot count scored −0.001657 — so the gain is the layout, not the smaller budget. H37-1 then replaced the content-blind packing rule itself: lazy-greedy maximum expected coverage of the detector probability field under the official 300 m kernel, at the same 41,333-px budget, kept the H27-4 blind flank prune, and passed a fresh 10-seed gate at +0.007289 over the d=2.8 reference (+0.004614 over H36-1, 10/10 seeds, 4/4 folds; content-blind matched-N control −0.044684), whose modelled live DTI of 0.278–0.286 did not survive its far-field falsification test: under LOSFO the rule ties the raster cascade (−0.000037 ±0.000832), so the projection is withdrawn and the one-click slot returned to H36-1 (0.2717–0.2727).

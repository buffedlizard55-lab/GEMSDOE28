Why thinning and pruning can help

For fixed truth size G, the stipulated Tversky expression can be written S = TP / (0.2·TP + 0.2·FP + 0.8·G). An addition improves it only when its marginal ΔTP/ΔFP > 0.2·S/(1−0.2·S). Deleting low-efficiency predictions reverses that tradeoff. Sparse dots may preserve triangular-kernel coverage while reducing redundant prediction mass.

This algebra is not a new score. The missing organizer truth prevents measuring the champion's actual TP/FP changes. Its reported advantage is plausibly emission hygiene; no causal score explanation or geothermal discovery is established. No candidate in this review is shown to exceed the user-supplied result.
